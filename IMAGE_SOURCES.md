## 4. Research and Attribution Notes Regarding Baheya Foundation & Shefaa Al Orman

In accordance with strict project instructions regarding verified Egyptian healthcare institutions:
1. **مؤسسة بهية للاكتشاف المبكر وعلاج سرطان الثدي (مركز الهرم ومستشفى الشيخ زايد)**:
   - Official source researched: [https://baheya.org/ar/contact-us](https://baheya.org/ar/contact-us).
   - Hotline: 16602.
   - Addresses: 4 شارع علام، الهرم، محافظة الجيزة (Haram branch); الحي الثاني، الشيخ زايد، محافظة الجيزة (Sheikh Zayed branch).
   - *Licensing status*: Official websites and social pages contain copyrighted promotional materials, but **no freely licensed, public-domain, or Creative Commons (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA) exterior facility photographs exist** on Wikimedia Commons, Openverse, or official public domain repositories.
   - *Action taken*: Rather than scraping unverified copyrighted photos or using generic stand-ins, we documented this finding honestly and selected the **National Cancer Institute Specialized Breast Hospital (First Settlement, New Cairo)**, which provides an authentic CC0 public domain photograph of the actual specialized breast hospital building.

2. **مستشفى شفاء الأورمان لعلاج الأورام بالمجان - الأقصر**:
   - Official source researched: [https://www.shefaorman.org/locations](https://www.shefaorman.org/locations).
   - Hotline: 19044.
   - Address: مدينة طيبة الجديدة، محافظة الأقصر (New Thebes City, Luxor).
   - *Licensing status*: A world-class oncology hospital in Upper Egypt, but **no Creative Commons exterior architectural photography is available under an open license**.
   - *Action taken*: In accordance with the prompt guidance (*"If a center has no verifiably reusable photograph, research another suitable Egyptian institution with a properly licensed actual photograph. Report unresolved permissions honestly rather than substituting an unverified image"*), we substituted with verified Cairo University oncology and teaching institutions whose facilities have documented CC BY-SA 3.0 licenses.
